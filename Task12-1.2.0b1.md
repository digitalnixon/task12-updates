# Task12 1.2 beta 1

This is a test build. Thank you for trying it. Please send any feedback to whoever shared it with you.

- **A new look.** Task12 is now blocky and monochrome, with a light mode. Use the **Dark / Light** switch at the top of the side panel; Task12 remembers your choice.
- **A bigger window on bigger screens.** Everything grows with the window, and the new slider above the images changes the tile size.
- **A quality chart.** It shows the trade-offs of High, Balanced, Smallest and Manual at a glance.
- **Smoother progress.** The rings and the new progress bar move evenly and never jump backwards.
- **Settings (⌘,).** Appearance, processing speed, the launch notice, notifications, where files are saved, and the preset for new images.
- **A notification when a batch finishes** while you're in another app, plus a progress bar on the Dock icon.
- **Results count down** to the new total size.
